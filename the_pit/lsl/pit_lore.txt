EXAMINE|C03|ANY|This was the chapel of Saint Clement. The Pit kept the kneeling and changed what you kneel to.

# == VOICES
VOICE|Somewhere in the Kennel a man counts aloud, in numbers, never in names.
VOICE|A quill scratches in the Press-Gang. The Wren never sleeps before the Book is dry.
VOICE|Old Keys whistles The Widow's Tooth as he walks the yard. He always stops at the same bar of the song.
VOICE|The Governor's chain creaks once, as if he had shifted his weight.
VOICE|From the Cistern, a bubble breaks the surface. Then another. Then none.
VOICE|A laugh at the guards' table, and a die rolls off it and keeps rolling, much too long.
VOICE|The rack's wheel clicks back one notch by itself.
VOICE|Low from somewhere below: ...and the tide comes in where the drowned men go.
VOICE|A rat runs the length of the channel and vanishes under the dais.
VOICE|The branding irons tick as they cool in the dark.
VOICE|Far above, a door closes. Every guard in the Pit straightens at once.
VOICE|The bell rope at the gate sways, though the air is still.
VOICE|A voice from the Stocks: Saint Clement, if you can hear me, I am sorry about your bell.
VOICE|A collar chain drags across the Kennel floor and stops at the end of its length.
VOICE|Water knocks on the culvert grate under the Cistern three times, like a visitor.
VOICE|In the guardroom someone reads the Sentence Board aloud, slowly, and then laughs.
VOICE|The kennelman's whistle, two notes, far off. Something in the straw answers.
VOICE|The cats on their pegs in the Mast stir, all twenty-seven tails at once.
VOICE|A coin rings on stone near the gate. When you look, it is a Crown coin, green with age.
VOICE|Scratch, scratch, scratch, scratch, and the long stroke across. Someone has finished a day.
VOICE|The tide turns. You feel it in the stones before you hear it.
VOICE|The banners behind the throne lift and settle, though no door has opened.
VOICE|Someone whispers your number. You did not know you had one yet.
VOICE|A crust drops down a light well and lands in the moonbeam. Somebody up there is laughing.
VOICE|In the throne hall the ship's wheel turns a spoke's width, as if the Pit had changed course.
VOICE|The Mute laughs. Nobody has ever heard the Mute laugh.
VOICE|A bone card skates out from under a grille, face up: the Ace of Hooks. When you look again it is gone.

# == BELLS
BELL|DAWN|One stroke from the gate bell. Grey seeps down the arrow slits and the dawn watch spits into the channel.
BELL|DAWN|Dawn. The Watch files under the Governor and touches its caps to him. He does not touch his.
BELL|DAWN|The dawn stroke. Gulls scream over the Widow's Tooth, and the night buckets go into the channel.
BELL|DAWN|First light. Old Keys walks the yard rattling every grille once, as the Crown's turnkeys did before him.
BELL|RATIONS|Two strokes: messes. Biscuit, salt fish and a dipper of water, weighed by the Purse to the crumb.
BELL|RATIONS|Two strokes. The slop bucket bangs down the yard toward the Kennel, and the dogs know the sound.
BELL|RATIONS|Messes. The Quartermaster counts the bowls going out and the spoons coming back.
BELL|RATIONS|Second mess: pease boiled grey and a heel of bread. The hooked men get a tot of rum, and make sure you smell it.
BELL|ROLLCALL|Roll call. Hask reads the Sentence Board. Numbers answer from every grille; the Kennel answers with barks.
BELL|ROLLCALL|Roll call. Tallies are counted against the chalk. Somewhere a man has one stroke too many.
BELL|ROLLCALL|Numbers, not names. A new voice gives a name, and the whole Pit goes quiet.
BELL|ROLLCALL|The Wren comes down with the Book under his arm. Every number is struck or kept.
BELL|LIGHTSOUT|Darken the Pit. The cell torches are banked low; only the yard lanterns and the Lord's braziers burn on.
BELL|LIGHTSOUT|Lights out. The keys go back on their hooks, and the dice come out.
BELL|LIGHTSOUT|Lights out. Moonlight comes down the light wells in white columns, and the cells count by it instead.
BELL|LIGHTSOUT|The last stroke. Tam Wicken's song goes round from cell to cell, low, until a guard bangs the bars.
BELL|HIGHTIDE|High tide. The sea shoulders up the channel, the Cistern rises, and every drain in the Pit breathes out.
BELL|HIGHTIDE|The tide is full. The Lord holds court: whoever kneels now is heard.
BELL|HIGHTIDE|High water. Spray comes through the arrow slits and salts the straw.
BELL|HIGHTIDE|The flood. The culvert under the Cistern booms like a drum, the way it boomed the night the Lord came back.
