THE PIT - INSTALL IN SECOND LIFE (fewest clicks)
================================================
Unzip ThePit_SL_A_model_and_scripts.zip and ThePit_SL_B_bulk_upload.zip into the same
folder. You get three folders: 1_model, 2_bulk_upload, 3_scripts.

ALREADY UPLOADED "EL FOSO V3"? Do NOT upload the model again: it is the same,
byte for byte. Skip step 1. Your materials with the old names (Piedra, Suelo...)
are recognised too, so in step 2 you only need the 12 pose animations (*.anim).
In step 4 pit_core removes the old foso_ scripts by itself and renames every
piece to English.

1. MODEL - one upload
   Build > Upload > Model... > pick 1_model/ThePit.dae
   The LOD and physics files (ThePit_LOD2/_LOD1/_LOD0/_PHYS.dae) load by
   themselves: keep the five files together. Leave scale at 1.0.
   Click "Calculate weights & fee", then "Upload".

2. MATERIALS AND EFFECTS - one upload
   Build > Upload > Bulk... > open 2_bulk_upload > Ctrl+A > Open > Upload.
   That is the 8 PBR materials (Stone, Floor, Wood, Iron, Cloth, Bone, Straw,
   Water), the 7 effect textures and the 12 pose animations (pit_lord,
   pit_rack, pit_dog...: looped, priority 4). The window shows the total price
   before you confirm.

3. REZ AND FILL
   Drag "The Pit" from your inventory onto the ground.
   Right-click it > Edit > Contents tab.
   Drag in everything you just uploaded: 8 materials, 7 textures and 12
   animations (newest items in your Materials, Textures and Animations
   folders; Ctrl+click selects several, then drag them all at once).

4. SCRIPTS - six of them, any order
   In the same Contents tab, for each file in 3_scripts:
   "New Script" > double-click it > Ctrl+A > paste the file > Save.
       pit_core   pit_control   pit_doors   pit_fire   pit_rooms   pit_throne

Done. Everything starts by itself: the pieces get their names and physics,
the materials are applied, the doors, fire and roleplay menus work.

Owner chat commands (channel 7):
   /7 settings   switch any feature on or off (also "Settings" on the throne)
   /7 help       every command
   /7 lowlag     keep the roleplay, drop the heavy effects
   /7 report     what every script found (send it if something fails)
   /7 seat down  while sitting: lower that seat 5 cm (also up, forward,
                 back, reset) - saved for everyone

Optional extras (drop into the same Contents): sounds named door_creak,
lock_clank, portcullis_chain, press_gang_bell, and any sound whose name starts
with "ambient". Without the animations, built-in SL poses are used.
