THE VILLA - INSTALL IN SECOND LIFE
==================================
Unzip Villa_SL_A_models_and_scripts.zip and Villa_SL_B_bulk_upload.zip into the same folder.
You get: 1_models, 2_bulk_upload, 3_scripts.

What you get: the Villa Core (36 x 36 m plinth, infinity pool with spa, sun loungers, round
daybed, fire-pit lounge, olive trees, garden lights and the Core totem) plus three houses it
turns into from a menu: NAUTILUS (white shell dome), DUNE (sand roof over an olive courtyard)
and NOCTURNE (dark dome, glowing bar). All mesh, PBR. About 90 LI for the Core + 115-136 LI
for the house that is out (one at a time): about 225 LI in total.

1. MODELS - four uploads (Build > Upload > Model...)
   Pick 1_models/Villa_Core.dae. The LOD and physics files (_LOD2, _LOD1, _LOD0, _PHYS) load by
   themselves: keep them together. Scale 1.0. "Calculate weights & fee", then "Upload".
   Do the same with Nautilus.dae, Dune.dae and Nocturne.dae. Keep those names (the Core uses
   them to pick each house's default materials).

2. MATERIALS, SOUNDS, POSES - one upload
   Build > Upload > Bulk... > open 2_bulk_upload > Ctrl+A > Open > Upload.
   Everything is called villa_...

3. THE TWO SCRIPTS - make them once in your inventory
   Inventory > + > New Script. Rename it  villa_house  , open it, Ctrl+A, paste
   3_scripts/villa_house.lsl, Save. Again for  villa_core  with 3_scripts/villa_core.lsl.

4. THE CORE
   Rez "Villa_Core" where you want the villa (the whole plot comes with it; rotate it as you like).
   Right-click > Edit > Contents tab. In your inventory type  villa_  in the search box, select
   everything (click the first, Shift+click the last) and drag it all onto the Contents tab.

5. EACH HOUSE (Nautilus, Dune, Nocturne)
   Rez it anywhere, Edit > Contents, drag the same villa_ selection onto it, close, then
   right-click > Take. (A stray villa_core inside a house deletes itself.)
   Finally select the three houses in your inventory and drag them onto the Core's Contents.

Done. Touch the Core totem (or any part of the villa): Houses > pick one. It appears in place
with its own materials. The other menus change everything live, on the house and the plot:
   Style     walls, floors, wood, stone, fabric, metal, glass, LED, pool water (35 PBR materials)
   Fabric    upholstery colour (12 tints)
   Lights    Day, Golden, Evening, Night, Party, Off; LED colour; brighter/dimmer
   Pool      water (turquoise, lagoon, black mirror), pool light colours, spa bubbles
   Ambience  garden, night, rain on glass, surf, chimes, lounge music, pool, fire; volume; fire on/off
   Doors     open/close (or touch a door)
   Settings  chat text on/off, sounds, particles, lights, access (owner/group/everyone),
             move the house (fine/coarse), reset
Sit on any sofa, chair, stool, lounger, bed or daybed; touch your seat to change pose.
